Section 1: Jurisdictional Foundations

The invocation of appellate jurisdiction under Article 136 of the Constitution requires substantive proof of legal arbitrariness. In procedural review, statutory mandates governing fundamental rights take absolute precedence over intermediate agency determinations.

A prima facie demonstration of non-application of mind invalidates executive detention without necessitating full discovery proceedings.
