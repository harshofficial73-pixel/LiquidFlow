Constitutional Jurisprudence & Fundamental Freedoms

The doctrine of non-arbitrariness guarantees protection under equal protection clauses across all sovereign executive actions. When statutory authorities issue directives without reasoned consideration, the action remains inherently vulnerable to judicial review.

Article 21 guarantees that no person shall be deprived of personal liberty except according to fair, just, and non-oppressive procedures established by law.
